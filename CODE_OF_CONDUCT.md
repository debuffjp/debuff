# Code of Conduct

debuff exists to give people more room to play and create. We want every contributor, jam participant and user to feel welcome, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on work, not on people. Be specific and constructive.
- Credit others' work, assets and ideas.
- Make room for newcomers, and help them find their footing.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or events.

## Scope

This applies in every debuff space: repositories, issues, chats, jams and events, and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers (see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) for the private contact channel). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, or warn, suspend or ban anyone whose behaviour violates this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
