The README and JSDoc point music and sound effects at plonk (`plonkjs`) on `audio.bus("music")`, with `player.sfx` for effects in the song's key. `synth` stays, documented as a placeholder. Before 0.29.2 they suggested `synth` for stylised SFX, which steered agents to blips.
