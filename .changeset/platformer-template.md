New official **platformer** template (`npm create debuff -- --template platformer`): buff runs and jumps through a Tiled level with tile collision, one-way platforms, coins, a goal, an automatically built atlas, coyote time, jump buffering and fully tunable movement. Official templates other than `basic` download on demand from the debuff repo, so they never grow the create-debuff package. `create()` and `resolveTemplate()` are new exports.
