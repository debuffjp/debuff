The template picker groups templates into base templates (starting points for your own game) and examples (finished small games). Four new base templates come with pixel art drawn in tenten: `topdown` and `isometric` (a tileset, buff with walk animations, pickups), `sidescroller` (run, jump, one-way planks) and `pointclick` (rooms, hotspots, an inventory, speech). Squish is listed as "Match 3". Templates declare `kind` in `template.json`.
