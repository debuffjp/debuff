The start-up splash shows the new DEBUFF logo on a slanted stripe, punching in and then "boiling" like hand-drawn animation (still for players who prefer reduced motion). The splash no longer opens debuff.jp on click or shows a URL: a click or tap only skips it, so games are safe on portals such as Poki and CrazyGames that forbid links out of the game.
